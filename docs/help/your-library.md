# Your library

Filtering your albums, artists and songs, Radio and mixes, the Archive, and how new music shows up.

## Filter chips

Albums, Artists and Liked Songs have a row of chips above the list. Type picks one kind of release: Albums, EPs and singles, Live, Compilations, or Demos and unreleased. Lossless shows music in lossless files, and Hi-res only files above 16 bits or 48 kHz. Decade picks one decade, and Added recently shows what came in over the last 30 days.

One chip from each group at a time, and the groups combine, so Hi-res with the 1970s shows hi-res records from that decade. An artist or song counts by its albums. A chip only shows when it would leave something to see. When the chips leave nothing, **Clear filters** takes them all off.

### Remember library filters

Settings, Library, Remember library filters (under Advanced) keeps the chips you picked when you come back or restart Platter. Turn it off and the pages open unfiltered each time Platter starts. It's kept on this computer.

## Radio and mixes

Radio and mixes has every mix Platter made from your listening on one page: Made for you, By mood and By genre. Open it from the sections list in Index, Turntable, Gatefold and Console. Mixes refresh as you listen, and the first ones appear within a day of playing a few albums.

To start radio, choose Go to song radio in a song's menu, or Go to artist radio in an album's or artist's menu. If the page says Mixes are off, **Open Settings** takes you to the switch.

## The Archive

The Archive lists your demos and unreleased albums, the ones Platter counts as not officially released, with the same chips for lossless, hi-res, decade and added recently. It's in the sections of every layout except Classic, and Deck has an Archive key. [Archive and release status](archive-and-release-status.md) explains how Platter decides what goes in it.

## Albums without a cover

An album with no cover art gets a printed sleeve instead of a blank square: its title set in type on a flat colour, with the artist on bigger covers. The colour comes from the title, so neighbouring sleeves differ. Small covers show just the first letter.

Add a cover on your server and it replaces the sleeve at the next sync. On a Plex server you own, the album's menu has Edit, Change cover too.

## New albums

On a Plex server, an album added to your library shows up in Platter on its own within a few seconds, with its artist, in Recently added and What's new. You don't need to resync. Platter still checks the whole library every hour, in case the server didn't say. Subsonic servers are checked on a timer instead (see [Connecting to your server](connecting.md#jellyfin-and-subsonic-servers)).
