# Layouts

The six ways Platter can arrange its window, how to switch between them, and each one's options.

## Picking a layout

The layout decides where things go: the player, your sections, the queue. The look paints them. You can change either without touching the other.

The first time you start Platter, Pick a layout and a look shows Index, Deck, Turntable and Gatefold. Press **More layouts** for Classic and Console. To change it later, open Settings, Appearance, Layout and pick a card. The switch happens at once, and each card's picture is drawn in the look you use.

If you've used Platter since before layouts, a card offers Try the Index layout. **Keep Classic** closes it for good. Settings, Appearance, Show the layout offer brings it back.

## The six layouts

Index puts your sections down the left, the player strip across the top and the queue in a drawer. It's the layout new installs start with, and it reaches Radio and mixes, What's new and the Archive from its sections.

Deck puts a faceplate across the top with a two-line display, the transport and a volume knob, with source keys and search under it. Lyrics open as a page of their own.

Turntable makes the player a column on the left: the record on its platter, the format plates, the transport and what's next. Up next stacks one sleeve per record, with a count when several songs in a row come from it.

Gatefold opens like a record sleeve. The left page is now playing: a big cover, a few lines of sleeve notes, and lyrics or credits, whichever you had open last. The transport runs along the spine, and the page you're browsing is on the right.

Console is for big libraries: a tree of sections and filters on the left, a dense table you can sort, a details pane on the right and a one-line status bar along the bottom.

Classic is the layout Platter had before, with your library on the left and the player bar along the bottom.

In a narrow window every layout folds up rather than squeezing. The Layout setting says how the one you picked does it.

## Layout options

Each layout's own options show under the cards when it's picked.

- Index: Player strip, Top or Bottom.
- Deck: Source key names, Shown or Icons only. Below about 1360 pixels wide the keys drop their icons so they all fit beside search.
- Turntable: Player column, Narrow, Medium or Wide. This one is kept on this computer.
- Gatefold: Page turn, On or Off. The page you go to slides in over the spine, or fades with reduced motion.
- Console: Table columns (artist, album, year, format, length and plays, after the name) and Pane widths. Drag the handles between the panes to resize them, and **Reset widths** puts them back. Both are kept on this computer.

**Change layout** next to the setting covers the finer options for any layout: the player's buttons, Home's shelves, page headers, song list columns, cover shape and motion.

## Making your own

Pick Custom and choose where each part goes: Sections and playlists, Search, Player controls, Now playing, Queue, Lyrics, Format and signal plates, Credits and sleeve notes, and Filters. Each can go Top, Left, Main, Right, Bottom, in a Drawer, or Not shown, wherever it fits. The page always holds your library. Left side and Right side set how wide those are.

Some changes would leave you stuck, like taking the player controls away. Those aren't saved, and the reason shows under the list.

## Going back

**Reset layout** puts the layout you're on back the way it came, options included. **Switch to Classic** goes straight back to Classic and keeps your look. Your layout is part of your synced settings, so other computers follow it.
